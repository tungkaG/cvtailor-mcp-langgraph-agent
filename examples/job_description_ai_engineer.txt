About Acme AI

Acme AI is a fast-growing startup building intelligent automation tools for enterprise customers. We leverage cutting-edge LLM technology to create AI assistants that help businesses streamline their workflows and make better decisions.

Role Overview

We are looking for an AI Engineer to join our growing team. You will design, build, and deploy LLM-powered applications that solve real business problems. This role involves working closely with product, data science, and engineering teams to deliver production-ready AI solutions.

- Develop APIs and backend services to serve AI models
- Integrate with databases and external services
- Write clean, tested, and documented code
- Participate in code reviews and technical discussions
- Stay current with AI/ML research and best practices

- Proficiency with SQL databases (PostgreSQL, SQLite)
- Experience building REST APIs (FastAPI, Flask, or Django)
- Familiarity with version control (Git) and CI/CD practices
- Strong problem-solving and communication skills

Nice to Have

- Knowledge of RAG pipelines and vector databases
- Contributions to open-source AI/ML projects
